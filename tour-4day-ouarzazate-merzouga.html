Overview

Embark on an incredible 4-day desert tour from Ouarzazate to Merzouga, designed for travelers seeking an authentic Sahara experience. Journey through breathtaking landscapes, ancient kasbahs, and traditional Berber villages, culminating in the golden dunes of the Sahara Desert.

Highlights

  • 01Explore the cinematic landscapes of Ouarzazate and its famous kasbahs
  • 02Traverse the lush Draa Valley, home to thousands of date palms
  • 03Experience a magical camel trek in the Erg Chebbi Dunes
  • 04Spend an unforgettable night in a luxury desert camp under the stars
  • 05Visit ancient kasbahs and learn about Moroccan history and culture

Itinerary

Ouarzazate · Agdz · Merzouga · Nkob · Ouarzazate

Day 1Ouarzazate to Agdz – A Journey Through the Draa Valley

Begin your journey in Ouarzazate, the “Gateway to the Desert,” and visit the Kasbah Taourirt, a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Head toward Agdz, traveling through the spectacular Draa Valley, where you’ll witness sprawling palm groves, traditional Berber villages, and scenic mountain landscapes. Overnight stay in Agdz at a charming guesthouse.

OvernightCharming guesthouse in AgdzMealsDinner
Day 2Agdz to Merzouga – Gateway to the Sahara

Travel deeper into the Moroccan desert, passing through Alnif and other small villages, across rocky desert terrain and oasis towns, before reaching the stunning Erg Chebbi Dunes in Merzouga. In the late afternoon, enjoy a camel ride through the towering dunes and witness a breathtaking sunset. Savor a traditional Moroccan dinner at a luxury desert camp and spend the night under the stars in the heart of the Sahara.

OvernightLuxury desert campMealsBreakfast · Dinner
Day 3Merzouga – Full Day Desert Exploration

Spend a full day exploring the beauty and culture of the Sahara Desert. Visit nearby nomadic communities, fossil sites, and picturesque oases, immersing yourself in the desert’s tranquil atmosphere. Discover the traditional way of life of Berber nomads, visit Khamlia Village to experience Gnawa music and culture, and explore the black desert and fossil-rich areas around Merzouga.

OvernightLuxury desert camp or riad in MerzougaMealsBreakfast · Dinner
Day 4Merzouga to Ouarzazate – Return Through the Desert

After enjoying a sunrise over the dunes, make your way back to Ouarzazate via Rissani, a historic trading town, and Nkob, known for its kasbahs. Experience the lively market of Rissani, one of Morocco’s oldest souks, stop for scenic views and photo opportunities in Nkob, and end the tour with picturesque views of the desert landscapes, returning to Ouarzazate by evening.

Overnight—MealsBreakfast
