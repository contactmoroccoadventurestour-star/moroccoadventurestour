Overview

The 5 Days From Errachidia to Marrakech via Sahara Desert is a complete journey from eastern Morocco to the red city of Marrakech through the Ziz Valley, Merzouga desert, Todra Gorges, Dades Valley, Ouarzazate, Ait Ben Haddou, and the High Atlas Mountains. This tour is designed for travelers who arrive in Errachidia and want a deeper Sahara experience before continuing to Marrakech.

Your journey starts in Errachidia, a city located near the Ziz Valley and the desert routes of southeastern Morocco. From Errachidia, the road continues through Erfoud, Rissani, and desert villages before reaching Merzouga, near the golden dunes of Erg Chebbi.

In Merzouga, you will enjoy camel trekking across the dunes, sunset views, dinner at the desert camp, and an overnight stay under the stars. You will also have a full day to explore the desert area, including Khamlia village, nomad families, palm areas, and the landscapes around Erg Chebbi.

After the Sahara experience, the route continues through Todra Gorges, Dades Valley, Rose Valley, Ouarzazate, and Ait Ben Haddou before crossing the High Atlas Mountains to Marrakech. This tour is ideal for travelers who want a slow and complete desert route from Errachidia with camel trekking, Sahara camp, desert exploration, valleys, kasbahs, and mountain landscapes.

Itinerary

Errachidia · Merzouga · Todra · Dades · Ouarzazate · Marrakech

Day 1Errachidia – Ziz Valley – Erfoud – Rissani – Merzouga Desert

Your 5 Days Desert Tour From Errachidia to Marrakech via Sahara Desert starts with pick-up from your accommodation or airport in Errachidia. We begin the journey by driving through the Ziz Valley, one of the most beautiful palm valleys in southeastern Morocco. Along the way, several stops can be made for panoramic views over the palm groves, oasis landscapes, and desert scenery. The route continues towards Erfoud, a town known for fossils and desert products. After Erfoud, we continue towards Rissani, a historic town linked to old caravan routes and traditional markets. Depending on the day, we can stop near the market area before continuing to Merzouga. By the afternoon, we arrive in Merzouga, near the dunes of Erg Chebbi. Dinner and overnight stay will be at a hotel or riad near the desert.

OvernightHotel or riad near the desertMealsDinner
Day 2Explore Merzouga Area – Khamlia – Nomad Families – Camel Trekking – Desert Camp

After breakfast, we start exploring the Merzouga area. The visit can include Khamlia village, known for its Gnawa music, and nearby desert areas with black volcanic landscapes around the dunes. You may also visit nomad families living around the desert region and learn more about their traditional way of life. The route continues through small desert villages, palm areas, and viewpoints around Erg Chebbi. In the afternoon, you will prepare for the camel trek. You will ride camels across the golden dunes and enjoy the sunset over the Sahara. After the camel trek, you will reach the desert camp. Dinner will be served at the camp, followed by a peaceful night under the stars. Overnight stay will be in a desert tent.

OvernightDesert tent in Erg ChebbiMealsBreakfast · Dinner
Day 3Merzouga Desert – Rissani – Erfoud – Todra Gorges – Dades Valley

Wake up early to enjoy the sunrise over the dunes before breakfast at the camp. After breakfast, we leave Merzouga and drive towards Rissani. Rissani is known for its traditional market, old trading history, and connection with desert caravan routes. After the visit, we continue through Erfoud and desert landscapes towards Todra Gorges. Todra Gorges are among the most impressive natural sites in southern Morocco, with high rocky cliffs and narrow canyon views. You will have time to walk through the gorge and enjoy the scenery. After visiting Todra Gorges, the journey continues through Tinghir and the valley road towards Dades Valley. Along the way, you will see palm groves, kasbahs, rock formations, and mountain views. Dinner and overnight stay will be at a hotel or riad in Dades Valley.

OvernightHotel or riad in the Dades ValleyMealsBreakfast · Dinner
Day 4Dades Valley – Rose Valley – Skoura – Ouarzazate

After breakfast, we leave Dades Valley and continue through the Rose Valley, known for its valley landscapes and local rose products. The road passes through small villages, kasbahs, and palm areas. The journey continues towards Skoura, an area famous for its palm groves and old kasbahs. Several stops can be made along the way for photos and panoramic views. After Skoura, we continue to Ouarzazate, often called the gateway to the desert. Depending on time, you can enjoy a short visit or relax at your accommodation. Dinner and overnight stay will be at a hotel or riad in Ouarzazate.

OvernightHotel or riad in OuarzazateMealsBreakfast · Dinner
Day 5Ouarzazate – Ait Ben Haddou – High Atlas Mountains – Marrakech

After breakfast, we leave Ouarzazate and continue to Ait Ben Haddou Kasbah. This ancient fortified village is one of Morocco’s most famous kasbahs and a strong example of traditional earthen architecture. You will have time to visit the kasbah, walk through its old alleys, and enjoy the surrounding views. After the visit, we continue towards the High Atlas Mountains. The road crosses the Tizi n’Tichka pass, with several stops for panoramic views, coffee, or short breaks. Along the way, you will pass Berber villages and mountain landscapes before reaching Marrakech. By the late afternoon or evening, we arrive in Marrakech. The tour ends with drop-off at your accommodation.
