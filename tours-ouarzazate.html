Tours from Ouarzazate

Ouarzazate Desert Tours – The Door to the Sahara

Ouarzazate sits where the High Atlas meets the desert, a city of earthen kasbahs and film studios with Ait Ben Haddou just down the road. From here, two great routes lead to the Sahara: east along the Dades and Todra gorges, or south through the palm groves of the Draa Valley. With Morocco Adventures Tour, every trip from Ouarzazate is private, starts at your hotel and is guided by a local team that knows both roads well.

Why start your desert trip in Ouarzazate?

Ouarzazate is already on the desert side of the Atlas. You skip the mountain crossing on day one and reach the gorges, the oases and the dunes sooner, then finish in Marrakech, Fes or back in Ouarzazate.

  • Pick-up from your hotel or riad in Ouarzazate
  • Private tours only — your own vehicle and driver
  • Two routes to the dunes: the gorges or the Draa Valley
  • Finish in Marrakech, Fes or back in Ouarzazate
  • A clear quote, with no hidden costs

What our Ouarzazate tours include

Each tour page shows exactly what is included. In general, our tours from Ouarzazate come with:

  • Private air-conditioned SUV or minivan
  • English or Spanish-speaking driver
  • Riads, guesthouses and a luxury desert camp
  • Breakfasts and dinners
  • A camel for each traveler in the desert
  • Pick-up and drop-off at your accommodation

What you will live on the way

Ait Ben Haddou

The UNESCO-listed earthen ksar seen in Gladiator, Lawrence of Arabia and Game of Thrones, with narrow lanes climbing to a granary at the top.

Kasbahs and film studios

The Taourirt kasbah, once home to the Glaoui family, and the Atlas Film Studios on the edge of the city.

Dades, Todra and the Draa

Red cliffs and rock formations in the gorges, or the long palm groves and kasbahs of the Draa Valley on the southern road.

The Erg Chebbi dunes

A camel ride at sunset, dinner and Gnawa music by the fire, and a night under a sky full of stars.

  • Film lovers and photographers
  • Couples and families looking for a private trip
