Overview

Experience Ouzoud Waterfalls day trip from Marrakech, where you’ll discover one of Morocco’s most beloved natural gems. The Ouzoud Falls, cascading from a height of 110 meters, offer fantastic photo opportunities and a chance to dive into refreshing river pools. You can also meet the playful local monkeys along the way. After soaking in the beauty of the waterfalls, treat yourself to a delicious Tagine at a nearby café while enjoying the magnificent view of the falls in the background!

Itinerary

Marrakech · Ouzoud Waterfalls · Marrakech

9:00 AMDeparture from Marrakech

Your journey begins with a convenient pick-up from your accommodation in Marrakech. Set off on a comfortable drive towards the Ouzoud Waterfalls, located around 2.5 hours away. Along the way, enjoy the scenic views of the Atlas Mountains and charming Berber villages.

11:30 AMArrival at Ouzoud Waterfalls

Upon arrival, you'll be greeted by the stunning sight of the 110-meter-high Ouzoud Falls. The lush surroundings and thundering cascades create a peaceful and awe-inspiring atmosphere.

12:00 PMGuided Walk and Monkey Spotting

Join a guided walk down the trail towards the base of the waterfalls. Along the way, learn about the local flora and fauna and encounter playful Barbary monkeys. Be sure to have your camera ready for some great photo opportunities.

1:00 PMFree Time and Swimming

Take some time to explore the area at your own pace. Relax by the river, enjoy the views, or take a refreshing swim in the natural pools at the bottom of the falls. The cool water is perfect for a dip on a warm day.

2:00 PMLunch with a View

Enjoy a delicious Moroccan meal at a local café with a spectacular view of the waterfalls. Savor traditional dishes such as Tagine, while soaking in the serene ambiance of the surrounding nature.

3:30 PMOptional Boat Ride or Leisure Time

After lunch, choose to take an optional boat ride that brings you closer to the foot of the falls for a unique perspective, or simply relax by the water, taking in the beauty of the scenery.

4:30 PMDeparture from Ouzoud

After a day filled with nature and relaxation, it’s time to head back to Marrakech. Enjoy the peaceful drive through the countryside as you reflect on the day’s adventure.

7:00 PMArrival in Marrakech

Arrive back in Marrakech, where you will be dropped off at your accommodation, concluding your memorable day trip to Ouzoud Waterfalls.

This itinerary offers the perfect mix of adventure, scenic beauty, and relaxation, making it an unforgettable escape from the city.
