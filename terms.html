Terms & Conditions

Last updated: November 2026

1. Who we are

Morocco Adventures Tour is a local travel team based in Morocco that organises private tours, day trips and desert activities. You can reach us by WhatsApp at +212 678 237 982 or by email at contact.moroccoadventurestour@gmail.com.

2. Quotes and bookings

Every trip is private and planned around your request. After you contact us, we send you a programme and a price. Your booking is confirmed once we have received your deposit and sent you a written confirmation by email or WhatsApp.

3. Payment

A deposit of 30% of the total price is required to confirm the booking, paid in advance by PayPal, bank transfer or Western Union. The remaining 70% can be paid in cash (EUR, USD, MAD or GBP) once you arrive in Morocco, on the first or the last day of the tour. You can also pay the full amount in advance. Card payments in Morocco and full payments by PayPal carry a 5% fee. Full details are on our Payment Policy page.

4. Cancellation by the traveller

If you wish to cancel your tour, we must receive written confirmation by email. If you cancel 3 days or more before the start of the tour, your payment is 100% refundable. If you cancel 2 days or less before the start, or do not show up, it is non-refundable.

5. Changing your date

Instead of cancelling, you can move your tour to another date once, at no extra cost. Your deposit is kept and can be used for a tour within 12 months of the original date, subject to availability.

6. Events beyond our control

If a trip is affected by events that neither you nor we can control, such as a cancelled flight, a sandstorm, a closed road or a decision by the authorities, we will work with you to find a solution: a new date, a change of route or another arrangement. Some parts of an itinerary may change on the day for safety reasons; we always choose the closest alternative.

7. What the price includes

What is included and not included in the price is listed on each tour page and in your written quote. Anything not listed as included, such as drinks, lunches, entrance fees, tips and personal expenses, is paid separately.

8. Documents and insurance

You are responsible for having a valid passport and any visa needed to enter Morocco. We strongly recommend travel insurance covering medical costs, cancellation and lost luggage.

9. Activities and safety

Activities such as camel rides, quad biking, buggy rides, sandboarding and hiking involve some physical effort and risk. Please follow the instructions of your driver and guides, tell us before the trip about any health condition that matters, and take part at your own judgement.

10. During your trip

If something is not as expected, tell your driver or contact us straight away on WhatsApp so we can fix it on the spot. Problems reported only after the trip are much harder to solve.

Invece di cancellare, puoi spostare il tour a un’altra data una volta, senza costi aggiuntivi. L’acconto resta valido e può essere usato per un tour entro 12 mesi dalla data originale, secondo disponibilità.
