Overview

Embark on a breathtaking 3-day desert journey from Ouarzazate to Marrakech. Discover ancient kasbahs, marvel at the beauty of the Sahara Desert, and traverse the stunning Atlas Mountains. This tour offers a perfect blend of Morocco’s history, culture, and natural wonders.

Highlights

  • 01Visit the cinematic city of Ouarzazate and its famous kasbahs
  • 02Experience the magic of the Sahara Desert with a camel trek
  • 03Spend a night under the starry desert sky in a luxury camp
  • 04Explore the UNESCO World Heritage Site of Ait Ben Haddou
  • 05Drive through the scenic High Atlas Mountains to Marrakech

Itinerary

Ouarzazate · Draa Valley · Merzouga · Dades · Marrakech

Day 1Ouarzazate to Merzouga – Gateway to the Sahara

Your tour begins in Ouarzazate, known as the “Hollywood of Morocco,” where you’ll explore its famous kasbahs and scenic surroundings, including Kasbah Taourirt, a historic fortified village. From there, travel through the picturesque Draa Valley, dotted with lush palm groves, striking oases and traditional Berber villages, before reaching the desert town of Merzouga. Arrive in Merzouga for a camel trek into the golden dunes of the Erg Chebbi Desert, and enjoy a traditional Moroccan dinner and music around the campfire in a luxury desert camp.

OvernightLuxury desert camp in MerzougaMealsDinner
Day 2Merzouga to Dades Valley – Gorges and Kasbahs

Wake up early to witness the mesmerizing sunrise over the dunes, then journey towards the stunning Dades Valley, passing through remarkable landscapes and fascinating sites. Visit the towering cliffs of Todra Gorge, one of Morocco’s most dramatic canyons, explore traditional Berber villages and kasbahs along the route, and drive through the Dades Valley, famous for its “Monkey Fingers” rock formations and scenic beauty.

OvernightCharming riad in the Dades ValleyMealsBreakfast · Dinner
Day 3Dades Valley to Marrakech – Ait Ben Haddou and Atlas Mountains

On the final day, head towards Marrakech, stopping to visit the legendary Ait Ben Haddou, a UNESCO World Heritage Site and historic fortress featured in many films and TV shows. Cross the High Atlas Mountains via the Tizi n’Tichka Pass, where panoramic views of valleys and peaks await, and arrive in the vibrant city of Marrakech, where your tour concludes.

Overnight—MealsBreakfast
