Overview

The most comfortable way to travel from Fes to Marrakech through the Sahara. With a first night in Midelt, the long drive south is split in two, so you arrive in the dunes rested and every day keeps time for walks, stops and photos.

From the cedar forests of the Middle Atlas you follow the Ziz Valley and the fossil town of Erfoud to Merzouga, where a camel carries you into the dunes of Erg Chebbi for a night in a desert camp. Then come Rissani, the Todra Gorge and a night in the Dades Valley.

The last two days follow the road of the kasbahs: the Skoura oasis, Ouarzazate, and Ait Ben Haddou on the way to the Tizi n’Tichka pass and Marrakech.

Itinerary

Fes · Midelt · Merzouga · Dades · Ouarzazate · Marrakech

Day 1Fes – Ifrane – Azrou – Midelt

Leave Fes in the morning and drive into the Middle Atlas. Walk the tidy streets of Ifrane, the alpine-style town known as the Switzerland of Morocco, then meet the Barbary macaques in the cedar forest of Azrou. Continue to Midelt, the apple town set between the Middle and High Atlas, for lunch, a relaxed afternoon and the night.

OvernightHotel or guesthouse in MideltMealsDinner
Day 2Midelt – Ziz Valley – Erfoud – Merzouga

Cross the Tizi n’Talghemt pass and descend into the Ziz Valley, a long ribbon of date palms and earthen villages, with stops at the viewpoints. In Erfoud, visit a fossil workshop to see the marine fossils polished from the local stone. In Merzouga, ride a camel into the Erg Chebbi dunes at sunset to your camp for dinner and Berber music under the stars.

OvernightDesert camp in Erg ChebbiMealsBreakfast · Dinner
Day 3Merzouga – Rissani – Todra Gorge – Dades

Wake early for sunrise over the dunes, have breakfast and return to Merzouga by camel or 4×4. Stroll through the souks of Rissani, with their spices, fabrics and crafts, then walk between the 300-metre cliffs of the Todra Gorge. Continue to the Dades Valley, with its kasbahs and rock formations, for dinner and the night.

OvernightRiad or guesthouse in the Dades ValleyMealsBreakfast · Dinner
Day 4Dades – Skoura – Ouarzazate

Drive along the road of the kasbahs to the Skoura palm oasis and visit Kasbah Amridil, one of the best-preserved in Morocco. In Ouarzazate, the desert’s film capital, visit the Taourirt kasbah or the Atlas Film Studios before dinner and the night in a riad.

OvernightRiad or hotel in OuarzazateMealsBreakfast · Dinner
Day 5Ouarzazate – Ait Ben Haddou – Marrakech

Start the day at Ait Ben Haddou, the UNESCO ksar seen in Gladiator and The Mummy, and walk its winding lanes in the soft morning light. Then cross the High Atlas over the Tizi n’Tichka pass, among peaks, terraced fields and Berber villages. Arrival at your riad or hotel in Marrakech in the afternoon.

Overnight—MealsBreakfast
