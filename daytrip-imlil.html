Overview

Discover life in the High Atlas Mountains by Imlil Valley Day Trip from Marrakech, Enjoy a hike through spectacular mountain valleys at the foot of Mount Toubkal. Enjoy lunch in a local Berber house while enjoying a relaxing day away from bustling Marrakech!

Itinerary

Marrakech · Imlil · Aremd · Imlil · Marrakech

8:30 AMPick-up in Marrakech

Explore Imlil Valley on a day tour from Marrakech! You will be picked up at your hotel in the morning at 8.30 AM by your driver and guide. We then drive to Toubkal National Park at the foot of Mount Toubkal, the highest peak in North Africa.

MorningImlil village

The little village of Imlil is where most trekkings start from in this area, and we too will start our walk from here.

Late morningWalk up to Aremd

We will walk up to the small village of Aremd, situated at an altitude of 1.900 meters. From here we have a beautiful view over the surrounding valley. On the way you will be able to see traditional Berber villages and Jbel Toubkal (4.167 meters).

MiddayLunch in a mountain gîte

After about 1 – 1,5 hours, we arrive at a traditional mountain gîte (guesthouse) to enjoy a wonderful lunch. From here we will have a spectacular view over the Mizane Valley.

5:00 PMDescent and return to Marrakech

After lunch you can explore the village a bit more, and we then start the descent of about 30 minutes back to Imlil. From there, your guide and driver will take you back to Marrakech and drop you off at your hotel around 5 PM.
