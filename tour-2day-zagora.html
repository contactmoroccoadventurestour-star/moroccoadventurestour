Overview

Short on time but dreaming of the desert? This two-day journey takes you from Marrakech into southern Morocco, across the High Atlas Mountains, past Berber villages, old kasbahs and palm valleys, all the way to the gateway of the Sahara.

The road climbs over the Tizi n’Tichka pass, one of the most spectacular mountain roads in the country, before reaching Ait Ben Haddou, a fortified earthen village listed by UNESCO and seen in many famous films.

From Ouarzazate you follow the Draa Valley, a long ribbon of palm groves and mud-brick villages, to Zagora. There you ride a camel at sunset and spend the night in a desert camp under the stars.

An ideal choice if you have only a few days in Morocco and still want to see its mountains, its kasbahs and a real night in the desert.

Itinerary

Marrakech · Ait Ben Haddou · Draa Valley · Zagora · Marrakech

Day 1Marrakech to Zagora

We pick you up in the morning in Marrakech and drive into the High Atlas over the Tizi n’Tichka pass, with stops for views over the mountains and Berber villages. Visit the kasbah of Ait Ben Haddou, then continue through Ouarzazate, the gateway to the desert, and along the palm groves of the Draa Valley. In the afternoon you reach Zagora, ride a camel at sunset to the camp, and enjoy dinner and a quiet night under the stars.

OvernightDesert camp in ZagoraMealsDinner
Day 2Zagora to Marrakech

Wake up early for the sunrise over the desert, then have breakfast at the camp. We drive back through the Draa Valley and Ouarzazate and cross the High Atlas again, with stops for photos, coffee and views. You arrive in Marrakech in the late afternoon and we drop you at your accommodation.
