8 Days Essential Morocco from Marrakech

  • Duration8 days · 7 nights
  • StartMarrakech
  • EndMarrakech
  • PriceOn request
Overview Itinerary Included Map Book

Overview

Eight days that bring together the essentials of southern Morocco: the energy of Marrakech, the High Atlas and its kasbahs, the dunes of the Sahara, the palm valleys of the Draa and the fresh air of the Atlantic coast.

After a first evening in Jemaa el-Fnaa, you cross the Tizi n’Tichka to Ait Ben Haddou and Ouarzazate, follow the Rose Valley to Dades, walk in the Todra Gorge and ride a camel into the Erg Chebbi dunes for a night in a desert camp.

The way back is a real journey of its own: the Saghro mountains and Alnif, Zagora on the old caravan route to Timbuktu, the Draa Valley, the walled town of Taroudant and finally Essaouira, with its blue boats and ocean sunsets, before returning to Marrakech through the argan country.

Itinerary

Marrakech · Ouarzazate · Dades · Merzouga · Zagora · Taroudant · Essaouira · Marrakech

Day 1Arrival in Marrakech

We welcome you on arrival and drive you to your hotel or riad. In the evening, discover Jemaa el-Fnaa, the great square of Marrakech, with its musicians, storytellers and food stalls — the perfect first taste of Morocco.

OvernightRiad in MarrakechMeals—
Day 2Marrakech – Ait Ben Haddou – Ouarzazate

Cross the High Atlas over the Tizi n’Tichka pass, among Berber villages and changing landscapes. Visit the UNESCO ksar of Ait Ben Haddou, seen in Gladiator and Game of Thrones, then continue to Ouarzazate for the night.

OvernightRiad in OuarzazateMealsBreakfast
Day 3Ouarzazate – Rose Valley – Dades

Visit the Taourirt kasbah in Ouarzazate, then take your time through the Rose Valley and its small Berber villages. End the day in the Dades Valley, among red rock formations and green fields, in a riad overlooking the valley.

OvernightRiad in Dades ValleyMealsBreakfast · Dinner
Day 4Dades – Todra Gorge – Merzouga

Walk along the river in the Todra Gorge, between limestone cliffs over 300 metres high. Continue to Merzouga and, at sunset, ride a camel into the Erg Chebbi dunes to your desert camp for a traditional dinner under the stars.

OvernightDesert camp in MerzougaMealsBreakfast · Dinner
Day 5Merzouga – Alnif – Zagora

Wake up for the sunrise over the dunes. After breakfast, cross the Saghro mountains with a stop in Alnif, the town of fossils, and arrive in Zagora, the old caravan town famous for its “Timbuktu, 52 days” sign.

OvernightHotel in ZagoraMealsBreakfast · Dinner
Day 6Zagora – Draa Valley – Taroudant

Follow the Draa Valley, the largest palm grove in Morocco, with its kasbahs and oases. In the afternoon reach Taroudant, the “grandmother of Marrakech”, and walk along its walls and through its relaxed souks.

OvernightRiad in TaroudantMealsBreakfast
Day 7Taroudant – Essaouira

Drive to the Atlantic coast and Essaouira. Stroll through its UNESCO medina of white walls and blue doors, visit the fishing port, taste fresh seafood and watch the sunset over the ocean.

OvernightRiad in EssaouiraMealsBreakfast
Day 8Essaouira – Marrakech

Return to Marrakech through the argan forests, with a stop at a women’s cooperative to see how argan oil is made. End your journey with a farewell dinner in a traditional Moroccan restaurant.

Overnight—MealsBreakfast · Dinner
