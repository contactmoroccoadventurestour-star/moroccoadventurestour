Overview

Discover the essence of Morocco on an unforgettable 4-day private tour from Casablanca to Marrakech, filled with enchanting landscapes, rich history, and authentic cultural experiences. This tour offers the perfect balance of adventure and relaxation, providing a comprehensive glimpse of Morocco’s charm and vibrant heritage.

The 4-day Morocco tour from Casablanca to Marrakech is a carefully curated journey that captures the spirit of Morocco’s diverse landscapes and cultural heritage. With visits to the Sahara Desert, ancient kasbahs, and lively cities, this tour offers something for everyone. Whether it’s watching the sunrise over the dunes, wandering through historic medinas, or tasting authentic Moroccan cuisine, this experience is bound to leave you with cherished memories.

This tour is ideal for travelers seeking a short yet immersive Moroccan experience. From the bustling city of Casablanca to the golden sands of the Sahara Desert and the vibrant streets of Marrakech, this journey combines Morocco’s iconic landmarks with the warmth of its local culture.

Itinerary

Casablanca · Rabat · Merzouga · Dades · Ouarzazate · Marrakech

Day 1Casablanca to Rabat – Exploring Morocco’s Coastal Gems

Your journey begins in the modern metropolis of Casablanca, where you’ll visit some of the city’s most iconic landmarks before heading to the historic capital of Rabat. Start your tour with a visit to the stunning Hassan II Mosque, one of the largest and most magnificent mosques in the world, overlooking the Atlantic Ocean. In Rabat, explore the Kasbah of the Udayas, a UNESCO-listed charming fortress with Andalusian gardens and narrow streets, and visit the Mausoleum of Mohammed V, an architectural masterpiece and a symbol of Morocco’s heritage. Spend the night in Rabat at a comfortable riad or hotel, enjoying the city’s tranquil atmosphere.

OvernightRiad or hotel in RabatMeals—
Day 2Rabat to Merzouga – Into the Heart of the Desert

Leave the coastal charm behind as you head south towards the Sahara Desert. Journey through the scenic Middle Atlas Mountains, passing cedar forests and traditional Berber villages, and marvel at the lush palm groves of the Ziz Valley as you approach the desert. By evening, reach Merzouga, the gateway to the golden dunes of Erg Chebbi. Embark on a camel trek across the dunes to a luxury desert camp, savor a traditional Moroccan dinner under the stars, and experience Berber music and storytelling around a campfire.

OvernightLuxury desert camp in MerzougaMealsBreakfast · Dinner
Day 3Merzouga to Ouarzazate – Kasbahs and Oases

Wake up early to enjoy a mesmerizing desert sunrise before continuing your journey toward Ouarzazate, Morocco’s cinematic city. Visit local nomadic communities to learn about their way of life, explore the dramatic Todra Gorge, where towering red cliffs frame a serene river, and drive through the scenic Dades Valley, known for its rock formations and ancient kasbahs. Arrive in Ouarzazate, home to the Taourirt Kasbah and several film studios. Relax and unwind in Ouarzazate, staying at a traditional riad or modern hotel.

OvernightRiad or hotel in OuarzazateMealsBreakfast
Day 4Ouarzazate to Marrakech – Through the High Atlas Mountains

Conclude your journey with a scenic drive through the High Atlas Mountains to Marrakech. Stop at Ait Ben Haddou, a UNESCO World Heritage site, a stunning ksar and filming location for famous movies, then cross the breathtaking Tizi n’Tichka Pass, with panoramic views of the surrounding mountains. On arrival in Marrakech, explore its vibrant streets, including the bustling Jemaa el-Fnaa square, lively souks, and historical landmarks like the Koutoubia Mosque.

Overnight—MealsBreakfast
