7 Days Sahara Desert Tour from Marrakech

Overview Itinerary Included Map Book

Overview

A full week to travel from Marrakech into the Sahara at an easy pace: mountain passes, earthen kasbahs, deep gorges, palm valleys and, at the heart of the trip, two nights among the golden dunes of Erg Chebbi.

After a first afternoon in the medina of Marrakech, you cross the High Atlas to Ait Ben Haddou and the Dades Valley, walk in the Todra Gorge and ride a camel into the dunes at sunset. A second desert day leaves time for sunrise, a nomad family and the Gnawa music of Khamlia.

The way back follows a different road: the Saghro mountains and Alnif, Zagora on the old caravan route, the long palm groves of the Draa Valley and Ouarzazate, before a last crossing of the Atlas and a farewell dinner in Marrakech.

Itinerary

Marrakech · Dades · Merzouga · Zagora · Ouarzazate · Marrakech

Day 1Arrival in Marrakech

We welcome you in Marrakech and take you to your riad. In the afternoon, walk through the medina and its souks of spices, leather and lanterns, and end the day in Jemaa el-Fnaa as the square fills with musicians, storytellers and food stalls.

OvernightRiad in MarrakechMeals—
Day 2Marrakech – Ait Ben Haddou – Dades Valley

Cross the High Atlas over the Tizi n’Tichka pass, with wide views over peaks and Berber villages. Visit the UNESCO ksar of Ait Ben Haddou, a backdrop for many films, then pass through Ouarzazate and continue to the Dades Valley and its red rock formations.

OvernightRiad in the Dades ValleyMealsBreakfast · Dinner
Day 3Dades – Todra Gorge – Merzouga

Walk along the river at the bottom of the Todra Gorge, between cliffs around 300 metres high. Continue across the desert plains to Merzouga, then ride a camel into the Erg Chebbi dunes at sunset to reach your camp for dinner under the stars.

OvernightDesert camp in Erg ChebbiMealsBreakfast · Dinner
Day 4A full day in the Sahara

Wake early for sunrise over the dunes. During the day, meet a nomad family to learn about life in the desert and visit Khamlia, a village known for its Gnawa music. Back at camp, enjoy a relaxed evening, dinner and a sky full of stars.

OvernightDesert camp in Erg ChebbiMealsBreakfast · Dinner
Day 5Merzouga – Alnif – Zagora

Leave the dunes and drive along the Saghro mountains to Alnif, a small town known for its fossils, with a lunch stop on the way. Arrive in Zagora, the old gateway to the caravan routes, and see the famous sign announcing 52 days to Timbuktu.

OvernightGuesthouse in ZagoraMealsBreakfast
Day 6Zagora – Draa Valley – Ouarzazate

Drive up the Draa Valley, one of the largest palm groves in Morocco, lined with Berber villages and old kasbahs. In Ouarzazate, visit the Taourirt kasbah or the Atlas Film Studios before dinner and a quiet night in a riad.

OvernightRiad in OuarzazateMealsBreakfast · Dinner
Day 7Ouarzazate – Marrakech

Cross the High Atlas one last time, with photo stops along the way. Back in Marrakech, close your journey with a farewell dinner in a traditional Moroccan restaurant.

Overnight—MealsBreakfast · Dinner
