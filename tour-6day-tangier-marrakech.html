Tangier to Marrakech

6 Days Tour from Tangier to Marrakech

  • Duration6 days
  • StartTangier
  • EndMarrakech
  • PriceOn request
Overview Itinerary Included Map Book

Overview

Embark on an unforgettable 6-day journey from Tangier to Marrakech, exploring Morocco’s most iconic destinations. From the blue streets of Chefchaouen to the historic alleys of Fes, and from camel trekking in the Sahara Desert to the vibrant souks of Marrakech, this road trip promises adventure, culture, and breathtaking landscapes.

Highlights

  • 01Wander the enchanting blue streets of Chefchaouen, the Blue City
  • 02Explore the historic Fes Medina, a UNESCO World Heritage Site
  • 03Experience the Sahara with a camel trek and a night in a luxury desert camp
  • 04Traverse the Middle Atlas Mountains, lush valleys, and dramatic gorges
  • 05Visit the ancient Ait Ben Haddou, a famous ksar and filming location
  • 06End your journey in the bustling, colorful city of Marrakech

Itinerary

Tangier · Chefchaouen · Fes · Merzouga · Dades · Marrakech

Day 1Tangier to Chefchaouen – The Blue Jewel

Your journey begins in Tangier, where you’ll travel to the stunning town of Chefchaouen, located in the Rif Mountains. Known for its iconic blue-washed streets, Chefchaouen offers a magical introduction to Morocco. Stroll through its charming blue medina, relax at Plaza Uta el-Hammam with a refreshing mint tea, and visit the peaceful Ras El Ma spring for panoramic views.

OvernightChefchaouenMeals—
Day 2Chefchaouen to Fes – Discovering Heritage

Depart Chefchaouen and head toward Fes, stopping at historical landmarks along the way. Explore the ancient Roman ruins of Volubilis with their intricate mosaics, visit the Bab Mansour gate and the Sultan’s stables in Meknes, then wander through the labyrinth of narrow streets, traditional workshops, and vibrant souks of the Fes medina.

OvernightFesMealsBreakfast
Day 3Fes to Merzouga – The Road to the Desert

Leave Fes early for a scenic drive to Merzouga, the gateway to the Sahara Desert, passing through picturesque mountain landscapes and Berber villages. Stop in Ifrane, the alpine-style town often called “Little Switzerland”, spot Barbary macaques in the lush cedar woods of Azrou, and enjoy the views of the sprawling palm groves of the Ziz Valley. In the Sahara, ride a camel into the golden dunes of Erg Chebbi and spend an unforgettable night in a luxury desert camp under the stars.

OvernightLuxury desert camp in MerzougaMealsBreakfast
Day 4Merzouga to Dades Valley – Through the Gorges

Wake up to a breathtaking sunrise over the dunes and travel through dramatic desert landscapes and lush valleys. Take a walk through the stunning Todra Gorge with its towering rock walls, then marvel at the “Monkey Fingers” rock formations and picturesque kasbahs of the Dades Valley.

OvernightDades ValleyMealsBreakfast
Day 5Dades Valley to Marrakech – Crossing the High Atlas

Continue your journey to Marrakech, passing through the High Atlas Mountains and stopping at key cultural landmarks. Visit Ait Ben Haddou, a UNESCO World Heritage Site and well-preserved ksar that has appeared in many movies and shows, drive through the Tizi n’Tichka pass, one of Morocco’s highest mountain passes, with breathtaking views, and arrive in Marrakech, the “Red City”, to explore its vibrant energy.

OvernightMarrakechMealsBreakfast
Day 6Exploring Marrakech

End your journey with a full day in Marrakech, a city rich in culture, history, and vibrant souks. Explore the bustling Jemaa el-Fnaa square filled with storytellers, musicians, and food vendors, admire the Koutoubia, the largest mosque in Marrakech and a stunning example of Moorish architecture, and relax in the tranquil Majorelle Garden with its exotic plants and fountains. Your journey concludes in the evening, leaving you with unforgettable memories of Morocco.
