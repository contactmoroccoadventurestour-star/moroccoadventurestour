Overview

A full loop through the south of Morocco: over the High Atlas to the Sahara and back through the palm groves of the Draa Valley. Five days that combine mountains, kasbahs, canyons, dunes and oases at a comfortable pace.

The first days follow the classic desert road — Tizi n’Tichka, Ait Ben Haddou, Ouarzazate, the Rose Valley, Dades and the Todra Gorge — to the dunes of Erg Chebbi, where you ride a camel at sunset and sleep in a desert camp.

The return is different and quieter: across the Saghro mountains and the fossil town of Alnif to the Draa Valley, the largest palm grove in Morocco, with a night in Agdz and a visit to the kasbah of Tamnougalt before Ouarzazate and Marrakech.

Itinerary

Marrakech · Dades · Merzouga · Alnif · Agdz · Ouarzazate · Marrakech

Day 1Marrakech – Ait Ben Haddou – Dades Valley

We leave Marrakech for the High Atlas and the Tizi n’Tichka pass, with views of Berber villages and terraced fields. Explore the UNESCO ksar of Ait Ben Haddou, then visit Ouarzazate and its Taourirt kasbah — and the Atlas film studios if time allows. Through the Rose Valley you reach the Dades Valley for dinner and the night.

OvernightHotel or riad in Dades ValleyMealsDinner
Day 2Dades – Todra Gorge – Merzouga

Walk along the river in the Todra Gorge, between cliffs 300 metres high. Continue through palm groves and Erfoud, known for its fossils, to Merzouga. At sunset, ride a camel into the Erg Chebbi dunes to your desert camp for dinner, Berber music and a night under the stars.

OvernightDesert camp in MerzougaMealsBreakfast · Dinner
Day 3Merzouga – Alnif – Draa Valley – Agdz

Sunrise over the dunes, breakfast at the camp and back to Merzouga by camel or 4×4. Cross the Saghro mountains with a stop in Alnif, the town of fossils, then reach the Draa Valley and its Berber villages and kasbahs. Night in a guesthouse in Agdz with a homemade dinner.

OvernightGuesthouse in AgdzMealsBreakfast · Dinner
Day 4Agdz – Tamnougalt – Ouarzazate

A slow morning along the Draa Valley, among endless palm trees and mud-brick villages, with a visit to the old kasbah of Tamnougalt. Continue to Ouarzazate for a relaxed afternoon — the Cinema Museum, a walk or lunch in a local café.

OvernightHotel in OuarzazateMealsBreakfast
Day 5Ouarzazate – Marrakech

Cross the High Atlas once more, with a last photo stop at the Tizi n’Tichka pass, and arrive in Marrakech in the late afternoon, where we drop you at your accommodation.

Overnight—MealsBreakfast
