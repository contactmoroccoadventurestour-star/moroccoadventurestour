Overview

The Morocco Student Group Tour From Tangier is a private educational journey designed for student groups who want to discover Morocco through culture, history, landscapes, cities, and desert experiences. This tour connects northern Morocco with the Sahara desert and Marrakech, offering a balanced route between learning, exploration, and travel.

Your journey starts in Tangier, one of Morocco’s most important northern cities. From Tangier, the route continues through the Rif Mountains towards Chefchaouen, the famous blue city known for its peaceful streets, mountain views, and traditional atmosphere.

The tour continues to Volubilis, Meknes, and Fes, where students can learn about Roman history, imperial cities, old medinas, traditional crafts, and Moroccan architecture. In Fes, the visit includes the old medina, souks, artisan workshops, tanneries, and historic monuments with a local guide.

After Fes, the journey crosses the Middle Atlas Mountains through Ifrane, cedar forest areas, Midelt, and the Ziz Valley before reaching Merzouga. In the Sahara desert, the group will experience camel trekking, sunset views, dinner at the desert camp, and an overnight stay under the stars.

The final part of the tour passes through Rissani, Todra Gorges, Dades Valley, Ouarzazate, Ait Ben Haddou, and the High Atlas Mountains before ending in Marrakech. The Morocco Student Group Tour From Tangier is ideal for school groups, university groups, and educational travelers looking for a structured Morocco itinerary with culture, history, desert, and local experiences.

Itinerary

Tangier · Chefchaouen · Volubilis · Meknes · Fes · Ifrane · Midelt · Merzouga · Todra · Dades · Ait Ben Haddou · Marrakech

Day 1Tangier - Rif Mountains - Chefchaouen

Your Morocco Student Group Tour From Tangier starts with pick-up from your accommodation, port, airport, or train station in Tangier. After meeting your driver, we leave the city and drive through northern Morocco towards the Rif Mountains. The road passes through mountain landscapes, small villages, and panoramic viewpoints before reaching Chefchaouen. This blue-painted town is one of the most peaceful places in northern Morocco and offers a good introduction to local architecture, daily life, and mountain culture. After arrival, the group will have free time to explore the old medina, walk through the blue streets, visit the main square, and take photos around the town. Dinner and overnight stay will be at a hotel or riad in Chefchaouen.

OvernightChefchaouenMealsDinner
Day 2Chefchaouen - Volubilis - Meknes - Fes

After breakfast, the group will have more free time in Chefchaouen before leaving towards Volubilis. The road passes through hills, villages, and open countryside. Volubilis is one of Morocco’s most important Roman archaeological sites. The group will have time to explore the ruins, old columns, mosaics, and historic remains that show the Roman presence in Morocco. After visiting Volubilis, we continue to Meknes, one of Morocco’s imperial cities. In Meknes, the group can visit the old medina area, Bab Mansour, and historical places linked to the city’s imperial past. After the visit, we continue towards Fes. Dinner and overnight stay will be at a hotel or riad in Fes.

OvernightFesMealsBreakfast · Dinner
Day 3Fes Sightseeing Tour With A Local Guide

After breakfast, the group will meet a local guide for a sightseeing tour of Fes, one of Morocco’s oldest imperial cities. The visit can include the old medina, narrow streets, traditional souks, historic gates, old schools, and artisan areas. Students will discover how traditional crafts are made, how the medina is organized, and why Fes is one of Morocco’s most important cultural cities. The visit may also include the famous tanneries and panoramic viewpoints overlooking the old city. Lunch can be taken inside or near the medina. In the afternoon, the group returns to the accommodation. Dinner and overnight stay will be in Fes.

OvernightFesMealsBreakfast · Dinner
Day 4Fes - Ifrane - Cedar Forest - Midelt - Ziz Valley - Merzouga Desert

After breakfast, we leave Fes and travel towards the Sahara desert. The route passes through Ifrane, known for its mountain atmosphere and clean streets. After Ifrane, we continue to the cedar forest near Azrou, where Barbary monkeys can sometimes be seen in their natural surroundings. This stop gives the group a short nature break before continuing through the Middle Atlas Mountains. The journey continues towards Midelt, then through the Ziz Valley, famous for its palm groves and panoramic views. Several stops can be made along the way for photos and short explanations about the region. By the afternoon or evening, we arrive in Merzouga, near the dunes of Erg Chebbi. Dinner and overnight stay will be at a hotel or riad near the desert.

OvernightHotel or riad near MerzougaMealsBreakfast · Dinner
Day 5Explore Merzouga Area - Khamlia - Camel Trekking - Desert Camp

After breakfast, we start exploring the Merzouga area. The visit can include Khamlia village, known for its Gnawa music, and nearby desert areas with black volcanic landscapes around the dunes. The group may also visit nomad families living around the desert region and learn more about their traditional way of life. The route continues through small desert villages, palm areas, and viewpoints around Erg Chebbi. In the afternoon, the group will prepare for the camel trek. Everyone will ride camels across the golden dunes and enjoy the sunset over the Sahara. A 4x4 transfer can be arranged as an alternative to camels. After the camel trek, the group will reach the desert camp. Dinner will be served at the camp, followed by a peaceful night under the stars. Overnight stay will be in desert tents.

OvernightDesert camp, MerzougaMealsBreakfast · Dinner
Day 6Merzouga Desert - Rissani - Todra Gorges - Dades Valley

Wake up early to enjoy the sunrise over the dunes before breakfast at the camp. After breakfast, we leave Merzouga and drive towards Rissani, a historic town known for its traditional market and old caravan history. After Rissani, we continue through Erfoud and desert landscapes towards Todra Gorges. These high rocky cliffs are one of the most impressive natural sites in southern Morocco, with time to walk through the gorge and enjoy the scenery. After visiting Todra Gorges, the journey continues through Tinghir and the valley road towards Dades Valley. Along the way, the group will see palm groves, kasbahs, rock formations, and mountain views. Dinner and overnight stay will be at a hotel or riad in Dades Valley.

OvernightDades ValleyMealsBreakfast · Dinner
Day 7Dades Valley - Ouarzazate - Ait Ben Haddou - High Atlas Mountains - Marrakech

After breakfast, we leave Dades Valley and continue through the Rose Valley and Skoura palm groves towards Ouarzazate, often called the gateway to the desert. The journey continues to Ait Ben Haddou Kasbah, one of Morocco’s most famous fortified villages and a strong example of traditional earthen architecture. This stop is useful for learning about kasbah architecture, old caravan routes, and traditional building methods. After the visit, we cross the High Atlas Mountains through the Tizi n’Tichka pass. Several stops can be made along the way for panoramic views, photos, and short breaks. By the late afternoon or evening, we arrive in Marrakech. The Morocco Student Group Tour From Tangier ends with drop-off at your accommodation.
