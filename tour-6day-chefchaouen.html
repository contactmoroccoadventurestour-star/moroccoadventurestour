6 Days from Marrakech to Chefchaouen via Sahara

Overview

From the red walls of Marrakech to the blue streets of Chefchaouen, this six-day journey crosses Morocco from south to north — mountains, kasbahs, desert, imperial cities and the Rif, all in one route.

You leave Marrakech over the Tizi n’Tichka pass in the High Atlas, visit the UNESCO kasbah of Ait Ben Haddou and follow the valleys of the south — Ouarzazate, the Rose Valley, Dades and the dramatic cliffs of the Todra Gorges.

In Merzouga you ride a camel into the Erg Chebbi dunes at sunset and sleep in a desert camp under the stars. Then the road climbs north through the Ziz Valley, Midelt, the cedar forests of the Middle Atlas and Ifrane to Fes.

A local guide shows you the medina of Fes, its souks, tanneries and monuments. On the last stage you visit Meknes and the Roman ruins of Volubilis before reaching Chefchaouen, the calm blue town in the mountains of the north.

Itinerary

Marrakech · Ait Ben Haddou · Dades · Merzouga · Fes · Volubilis · Chefchaouen

Day 1Marrakech – Ait Ben Haddou – Dades Valley

We pick you up at your accommodation in Marrakech and drive into the High Atlas over the Tizi n’Tichka pass, stopping for views and Berber villages. Visit the kasbah of Ait Ben Haddou, famous for its earthen architecture, then continue through Ouarzazate, Skoura and the Rose Valley to the Dades Valley for dinner and the night.

OvernightHotel or riad in Dades ValleyMealsDinner
Day 2Dades – Todra Gorges – Merzouga

After breakfast we drive to the Todra Gorges and walk between its high rock walls. The road continues through Tinjadad and Erfoud to Merzouga, at the edge of the Erg Chebbi dunes. Ride a camel into the dunes for the sunset, then reach your camp for dinner and a quiet night under the stars.

OvernightDesert camp in MerzougaMealsBreakfast · Dinner
Day 3Merzouga – Ziz Valley – Ifrane – Fes

Wake up for the sunrise over the dunes and have breakfast at the camp. We head north via the market town of Rissani, the palm groves of the Ziz Valley, Errachidia and Midelt, then cross the Middle Atlas through cedar forests and Ifrane before arriving in Fes in the evening.

OvernightHotel or riad in FesMealsBreakfast · Dinner
Day 4Fes with a local guide

A full day in Fes, one of Morocco’s oldest imperial cities, with a local guide: the medina’s narrow streets, souks, historic gates, medersas and artisan quarters, including the famous tanneries. In the afternoon, enjoy a viewpoint over the medina before returning to your riad.

OvernightHotel or riad in FesMealsBreakfast · Dinner
Day 5Fes – Meknes – Volubilis – Chefchaouen

We leave Fes for Meknes, the imperial city of great gates and walls, then visit Volubilis, the Roman ruins set in open countryside. The road continues through green hills to Chefchaouen, where you arrive in the afternoon or evening.

OvernightHotel or riad in ChefchaouenMealsBreakfast · Dinner
Day 6Free time in Chefchaouen

After breakfast, explore Chefchaouen at your own pace: the blue alleys, small squares, local shops and mountain views. Your tour ends with drop-off at your accommodation in Chefchaouen.

Overnight—MealsBreakfast
