Overview

Embark on an incredible 3-day journey from Ouarzazate to Fes, where history and nature intertwine beautifully. Explore ancient kasbahs, enjoy a magical camel trek in the Sahara Desert, and traverse the Middle Atlas Mountains to the cultural heart of Morocco, Fes.

Highlights

  • 01Visit Ouarzazate’s iconic kasbahs and scenic landscapes
  • 02Experience the beauty of the Erg Chebbi Dunes with a camel ride
  • 03Spend a night in a luxury desert camp under a star-filled sky
  • 04Explore the stunning Todra Gorge and lush valleys
  • 05Drive through the breathtaking Middle Atlas Mountains

Itinerary

Ouarzazate · Draa Valley · Merzouga · Dades · Fes

Day 1Ouarzazate to Merzouga – Gateway to the Sahara

Begin your journey in Ouarzazate, where you’ll explore ancient kasbahs and enjoy scenic views. Visit the famous Kasbah Taourirt, then travel through the picturesque Draa Valley, known for its palm groves, lush oases and traditional Berber villages, before reaching Merzouga, the gateway to the Sahara Desert. Enjoy a camel trek into the golden Erg Chebbi dunes, and savor a traditional Moroccan dinner and music around a campfire at a luxury desert camp.

OvernightLuxury desert camp in MerzougaMealsDinner
Day 2Merzouga to Dades Valley – Stunning Gorges and Valleys

After witnessing a magical sunrise over the dunes and enjoying breakfast at the camp, travel to the breathtaking Todra Gorge and the scenic Dades Valley. This day is filled with extraordinary natural beauty and cultural exploration. Visit the towering cliffs of Todra Gorge, a favorite for hikers and climbers, and explore the Dades Valley, known for its dramatic rock formations and ancient kasbahs.

OvernightTraditional riad in the Dades ValleyMealsBreakfast · Dinner
Day 3Dades Valley to Fes – Journey Through the Middle Atlas

On the final day, leave the Dades Valley and travel through the Middle Atlas Mountains on your way to Fes. Along the route, enjoy panoramic views of cedar forests, charming towns, and fertile landscapes. Travel through scenic mountain passes and traditional Berber villages, pass by the cedar forests of Azrou, home to wild monkeys, and arrive in the historic city of Fes, the cultural and spiritual capital of Morocco.

Overnight—MealsBreakfast
