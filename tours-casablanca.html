Tours from Casablanca

Casablanca Tours – From the Atlantic to the Sahara

Most international flights to Morocco land in Casablanca, which makes it the natural first stop of a journey through the country. From here the road leads north to Rabat and the blue streets of Chefchaouen, east to the medina of Fes and the dunes of Merzouga, and south to the kasbahs and Marrakech. With Morocco Adventures Tour, every trip from Casablanca is private, planned around your flights and guided by a local team from start to finish.

Why start your trip in Casablanca?

Casablanca is Morocco’s largest city and its main air gateway. Starting here lets you see the Hassan II Mosque on day one and then travel through the whole country without backtracking.

  • Pick-up at Mohammed V airport or your hotel
  • Private tours only — your own vehicle and driver-guide
  • Imperial cities, the blue city and the Sahara in one trip
  • Tours from 4 to 12 days, fully adaptable
  • A clear quote, with no hidden costs

What our Casablanca tours include

Each tour page shows exactly what is included. In general, our tours from Casablanca come with:

  • Private air-conditioned SUV or minivan
  • English or Spanish-speaking driver
  • Hand-picked hotels, riads and a desert camp
  • Daily breakfast
  • A camel for each traveler in the desert
  • Itinerary adapted to your flight times

What you will live on the way

The Hassan II Mosque

Built partly over the Atlantic, with carved cedar, marble and zellige, it is one of the few mosques in Morocco open to visitors on guided tours.

Imperial cities and old medinas

From the Kasbah of the Udayas in Rabat to the maze of Fes el-Bali, discover the palaces, madrasas and souks of royal Morocco.

Chefchaouen, the blue city

Blue-washed lanes in the Rif Mountains, a calm square below the kasbah and one of the most photographed towns in Morocco.

The Sahara at Merzouga

Ride a camel into the Erg Chebbi dunes at sunset and spend the night in a desert camp with dinner and Berber music.
