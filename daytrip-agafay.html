Overview

Agafay Day Trip offers guests with a variety of thrilling things to enjoy. Our company promises to provide a wide range of ideas to suit their clients’ preferences. Accompanied by your professional driver guide, you will traverse the beautiful Agafay desert and experience an exciting trip via extensive desert trails with profound lows and highs.

Itinerary

Marrakech · Agafay Desert · Marrakech

9:00 AMDeparture from Marrakech

Your adventure begins with a pick-up from your accommodation in Marrakech. Set out on a comfortable drive towards the stunning Agafay Desert, located just 45 minutes from the city. Enjoy the scenic journey through small Berber villages and picturesque landscapes.

9:45 AMArrival in Agafay Desert

Upon arrival in the vast Agafay Desert, you’ll be captivated by the rocky terrain and rolling hills. Although not a typical sand desert, Agafay offers a unique experience with its lunar-like landscape. Begin by taking in the breathtaking views of the Atlas Mountains in the distance.

10:00 AMCamel Ride Experience

Start your day with a traditional camel ride through the desert. Dressed in a blue turban and robe (optional), you’ll feel like a true nomad as you journey through the serene, rocky plains of Agafay. This leisurely ride offers plenty of photo opportunities and a chance to soak in the peaceful ambiance of the desert.

11:30 AMQuad Biking Adventure (Optional)

For those seeking more excitement, take an optional quad biking tour through the desert. Navigate the rough terrain and feel the thrill as you explore deeper into Agafay’s untouched beauty. Experienced guides will lead you through safe trails, making this an adventure to remember.

1:00 PMLunch in a Desert Camp

Enjoy a delicious traditional Moroccan lunch at a desert camp. Savor local dishes like tagine, couscous, and salads, served in a traditional Berber tent. As you dine, take in the tranquil desert surroundings and enjoy the hospitality of your hosts.

2:30 PMFree Time for Relaxation

After lunch, relax and soak up the desert atmosphere. You can unwind in the lounge area of the camp, sip mint tea, or take a leisurely walk around the camp to enjoy the quiet and stillness of the desert.

3:30 PMDeparture from Agafay Desert

After a memorable day in the Agafay Desert, it’s time to head back to Marrakech. On your way, enjoy one last look at the striking landscapes that surround you.

4:30 PMReturn to Marrakech

Arrive back in Marrakech, where you’ll be dropped off at your accommodation, concluding your Agafay day trip.

This itinerary offers a perfect balance of adventure, cultural experiences, and relaxation, making it an unforgettable day in the unique Agafay Desert.
