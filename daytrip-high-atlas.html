Overview

This High Atlas Mountains day trip from Marrakech to Ait Ben Haddou Kasbah is perfect for travelers short on time but eager to experience the magic of Morocco’s desert landscape. Explore the iconic Ait Ben Haddou, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, famously featured in numerous Hollywood blockbusters.

Travel in comfort by 4WD or minivan, following ancient caravan routes through the High Atlas range. Along the way, you’ll pass through lush oases, impressive kasbahs, and fortified Berber villages. Admire the architectural wonder of Ait Ben Haddou, a cultural treasure recognized by UNESCO and often used as a backdrop for movies.

Next, visit the historic Taourirt Kasbah in Ouarzazate, a grand fortified residence with a rich past. During your visit, you’ll have the opportunity to try camel riding for a bit of fun and laughter, experiencing the warmth and hospitality of the Moroccan desert (camel riding not included in the price).

Discover the breathtaking beauty of the High Atlas Mountains on this unforgettable day trip from Marrakech to Ait Ben Haddou.

Itinerary

Marrakech · Tizi n’Tichka · Ait Ben Haddou · Ouarzazate · Marrakech

7:30 AMPick-Up from Marrakech

Your adventure begins with an early morning pick-up from your accommodation in Marrakech. Set out on a scenic drive in a comfortable 4WD or minivan, heading towards the High Atlas Mountains. Enjoy views of traditional Berber villages and the stunning landscape as you travel along the historic caravan routes.

9:30 AMTizi n’Tichka Pass

Cross the winding Tizi n’Tichka Pass, one of the highest mountain passes in Morocco (2,260 m). Take in panoramic views of the surrounding mountains and valleys, with opportunities to stop for photos and admire the breathtaking scenery.

11:00 AMArrival at Ait Ben Haddou Kasbah

Arrive at Ait Ben Haddou, a UNESCO World Heritage Site and one of Morocco’s most iconic landmarks. This ancient fortified village, made entirely of clay, has served as the backdrop for many famous films and TV series. Explore its narrow streets and climb to the top for a stunning view of the surrounding desert and palm groves.

12:30 PMFree Time for Exploration

Spend some free time exploring Ait Ben Haddou at your own pace. Wander through its historic alleys, visit the local shops, or take photos of this extraordinary site.

1:30 PMLunch at a Local Restaurant

Enjoy a traditional Moroccan lunch at a local restaurant near Ait Ben Haddou. Savor dishes such as Tagine, couscous, and fresh salads while enjoying the relaxed desert atmosphere.

3:00 PMVisit Taourirt Kasbah in Ouarzazate

Head to Ouarzazate to visit the grand Taourirt Kasbah, a historic fortified residence that once belonged to the powerful Glaoui family. Take a guided tour of the kasbah, marveling at its intricate architecture and learning about its importance in Moroccan history.

4:00 PMOptional Camel Ride (Not Included in Price)

Before heading back to Marrakech, enjoy an optional camel ride for a bit of fun. Experience the traditional mode of desert transport, riding through the desert landscape while soaking in the beauty of the surrounding landscape.

4:30 PMDeparture for Marrakech

After a day of exploring the High Atlas Mountains and its treasures, start your journey back to Marrakech. Relax during the comfortable drive through the scenic landscapes.

7:00 PMReturn to Marrakech

Arrive back in Marrakech and be dropped off at your accommodation, bringing your High Atlas Mountains day trip to a close.

This itinerary offers a perfect blend of culture, history, and adventure, making it a memorable experience in Morocco’s High Atlas region
