Overview

Embark on a 6-day Morocco itinerary from Casablanca that takes you through the country’s most iconic cities and breathtaking landscapes. From the blue streets of Chefchaouen to the historic sights of Fes and the vast dunes of the Sahara Desert, this tour offers an unforgettable experience of Morocco’s culture, history, and natural beauty.

This itinerary is perfect for travelers who wish to see the highlights of Morocco in a short time. You will explore both the bustling cities and the quiet, serene desert landscapes, each offering a unique experience. This tour is ideal for those interested in history, architecture, nature, and authentic Moroccan experiences.

Itinerary

Casablanca · Chefchaouen · Fes · Merzouga · Ouarzazate · Marrakech

Day 1Casablanca to Chefchaouen – Arrival in the Blue City

Your Moroccan adventure begins in Casablanca, a modern city with a rich history. After a quick exploration of its main attractions, you’ll set off toward Chefchaouen, known for its striking blue-painted buildings nestled in the Rif Mountains. Admire the grandeur of the Hassan II Mosque, one of the largest mosques in the world, located by the Atlantic Ocean. Arrive in Chefchaouen, a serene town where every corner seems to be painted in shades of blue. Walk through the labyrinthine streets, visit the local markets, and explore the Kasbah and Ras El Ma Waterfall. Spend the night in a cozy riad, offering a peaceful escape surrounded by the mountain backdrop.

OvernightCozy riad in ChefchaouenMeals—
Day 2Chefchaouen to Fes – The City of Culture and History

Travel from Chefchaouen to Fes, Morocco’s cultural and intellectual hub. Known for its ancient medina, Fes is a city that feels like stepping back in time. Stop at Volubilis, a well-preserved Roman city where you can explore mosaics and ancient structures. Continue to Fes, where you’ll take a guided tour of its vibrant medina, a UNESCO World Heritage site, and visit Al-Qarawiyyin University and Bou Inania Madrasa, two of Fes’s most historic institutions, showcasing Islamic architecture and history. Stay in a riad within the medina to soak up the atmosphere of Fes, enjoying local Moroccan cuisine for dinner.

OvernightRiad in the Fes medinaMealsBreakfast · Dinner
Day 3Fes to Merzouga – Journey to the Sahara

Leave Fes behind and head towards Merzouga, located at the edge of the Sahara Desert. The drive will take you through diverse landscapes, from the Middle Atlas mountains to the vast expanses of the desert. Visit the picturesque town of Ifrane, known as “Little Switzerland” for its alpine-style architecture, and stop in the Ziz Valley to enjoy the lush oasis landscapes with palm groves and impressive gorges. Reach Merzouga, where you will begin your adventure into the Sahara Desert. Take a camel trek into the Erg Chebbi dunes and spend the night at a desert camp under the starry sky. Enjoy a traditional Berber dinner, followed by music and stargazing.

OvernightDesert camp in Erg ChebbiMealsBreakfast · Dinner
Day 4Merzouga to Ouarzazate – Kasbahs and Valleys

After a memorable desert experience, continue your journey to Ouarzazate, known as the gateway to the Sahara. Along the way, explore fascinating kasbahs and the dramatic landscapes of southern Morocco. Visit the Todra Gorge, a narrow canyon with towering cliff walls, an incredible spot for photos and short walks. Continue through the Dades Valley, known for its picturesque landscapes and traditional kasbahs, and arrive in Ouarzazate, famous for its Taourirt Kasbah and as a filming location for many international movies. Enjoy an overnight stay in a traditional guesthouse in Ouarzazate.

OvernightTraditional guesthouse in OuarzazateMealsBreakfast
Day 5Ouarzazate to Marrakech – Crossing the Atlas Mountains

Head to Marrakech, passing through the stunning High Atlas Mountains. This is a scenic drive filled with breathtaking views of rugged landscapes, Berber villages, and valleys. Visit the famous Ait Ben Haddou, a UNESCO World Heritage site, known for its well-preserved kasbahs and its role in numerous films, and drive through the Tizi n’Tichka Pass, a high-altitude route offering panoramic views of the Atlas Mountains. Reach Marrakech in the afternoon, where you can take a guided tour of the medina, including the Jemaa el-Fna square, Koutoubia Mosque, and the Bahia Palace. Spend the night in a beautiful riad or hotel in the heart of Marrakech.

OvernightRiad or hotel in MarrakechMealsBreakfast
Day 6Marrakech – Full Day Exploration

Your final day will be spent exploring Marrakech, one of Morocco’s most vibrant and historic cities. Visit the lively Jemaa el-Fna Square, where you can see street performers, food stalls, and local crafts, discover the famous Majorelle Garden and the museum dedicated to fashion designer Yves Saint Laurent, and wander through the colorful souks, offering everything from spices to textiles and handcrafted goods. In the late afternoon or evening, your 6-day tour will conclude, and you will be transferred to the airport or train station for your onward journey.
