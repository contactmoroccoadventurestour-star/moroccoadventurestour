Overview

Embark on a breathtaking journey through Morocco in just five days, as you explore the beautiful blue city of Chefchaouen, the historic city of Fes, and the stunning Sahara Desert. This 5-day Morocco itinerary from Casablanca offers a perfect balance of natural wonders, rich culture, and ancient history.

For travelers who want to experience the best of Morocco in a short time, this itinerary is ideal. Whether you’re drawn to the vibrant streets of Fes, the tranquil charm of Chefchaouen, or the majestic dunes of the Sahara Desert, this tour offers a diverse range of experiences.

Itinerary

Casablanca · Chefchaouen · Fes · Merzouga · Ouarzazate · Casablanca

Day 1Casablanca to Chefchaouen – A Journey to the Blue City

Your Moroccan adventure begins in Casablanca, Morocco’s economic hub, where you will visit some iconic landmarks before heading to the charming city of Chefchaouen. Visit the spectacular Hassan II Mosque, one of the largest mosques in the world, standing proudly by the Atlantic Ocean. In the afternoon, you’ll arrive in Chefchaouen, a small city nestled in the Rif Mountains, renowned for its blue-painted buildings and narrow streets. Stroll through its winding alleys, soaking in the sights, sounds, and unique atmosphere of the city. Spend the night in a cozy riad or hotel, where you can relax and reflect on the beauty of Chefchaouen.

OvernightRiad or hotel in ChefchaouenMeals—
Day 2Chefchaouen to Fes – Exploring Ancient History

Head from Chefchaouen to Fes, one of Morocco’s oldest and most important cities, known for its rich history, beautiful architecture, and medieval charm. Stop at the ancient Roman ruins of Volubilis, a UNESCO World Heritage site where you’ll explore well-preserved mosaics and architectural remnants. Continue to Fes, where you’ll enjoy a panoramic view of the city upon arrival before diving into its historic heart. Discover the maze-like streets of the Fes medina, a UNESCO World Heritage site that feels like stepping back in time, and visit the famous Al-Qarawiyyin University and the Bou Inania Madrasa. Stay in a charming riad within the medina to fully immerse yourself in Fes’s captivating atmosphere.

OvernightRiad in the Fes medinaMealsBreakfast
Day 3Fes to Merzouga – A Desert Adventure Awaits

Leave the cultural richness of Fes behind and embark on your desert adventure as you head towards the Sahara Desert. Stop by the picturesque town of Ifrane, known as “Little Switzerland” for its alpine-style architecture and cool climate, then continue through the Ziz Valley, a lush oasis surrounded by towering desert mountains, known for its palm groves and vast date plantations. Arrive in Merzouga in the late afternoon and take a camel trek across the towering dunes of Erg Chebbi to your desert camp. Enjoy a traditional Berber dinner in the camp, followed by stargazing and Berber music.

OvernightDesert camp in Erg ChebbiMealsBreakfast · Dinner
Day 4Merzouga to Ouarzazate – Exploring Kasbahs and Valleys

Wake up early to watch a magical desert sunrise, then continue your journey to Ouarzazate, the gateway to the southern Sahara, known for its dramatic landscapes and historic kasbahs. Visit the Todra Gorge, a stunning canyon with towering cliff walls that offer breathtaking views and a chance to take a short walk or explore the area. Drive through the Dades Valley, often called the “Valley of a Thousand Kasbahs,” with its vast landscapes and unique architectural structures. Arrive in Ouarzazate, famous for its Taourirt Kasbah and nearby film studios, where many Hollywood blockbusters have been filmed. Overnight in a traditional guesthouse or hotel in Ouarzazate, enjoying the peaceful surroundings.

OvernightGuesthouse or hotel in OuarzazateMealsBreakfast
Day 5Ouarzazate to Casablanca – Back to the Modern World

On the final day, you will make your way back to Casablanca, passing through the breathtaking High Atlas Mountains. Stop at Ait Ben Haddou, an ancient mud-brick village and UNESCO World Heritage site that has been featured in numerous films, including Gladiator and Game of Thrones. Travel through the Tizi n’Tichka Pass, one of the highest roads in Morocco, with panoramic views of the Atlas Mountains. Arrive back in Casablanca by evening, where you can relax or explore the city further before your departure.

Overnight—MealsBreakfast
