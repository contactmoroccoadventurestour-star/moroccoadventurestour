Overview

Embark on a 7-day Morocco itinerary from Casablanca and experience the essence of this fascinating country. From the modern cityscape of Casablanca to the ancient medinas of Fes and Marrakech and the dazzling dunes of the Sahara Desert, this journey will immerse you in Morocco’s culture, history, and natural beauty.

A 7-day tour offers a comprehensive and in-depth experience of Morocco’s diversity. This itinerary covers everything from vibrant cities, ancient history, and architectural marvels to breathtaking natural landscapes and desert adventures. Whether you’re a history buff, a foodie, or a nature enthusiast, this tour will ensure you experience the best of Morocco in one unforgettable week.

Itinerary

Casablanca · Rabat · Chefchaouen · Fes · Merzouga · Ouarzazate · Marrakech

Day 1Casablanca – The Gateway to Morocco

Begin your journey in Casablanca, Morocco’s economic capital and one of the largest cities in North Africa. While it’s a modern metropolis, it’s also home to historical landmarks and beautiful coastal views. Start with a visit to the Hassan II Mosque, one of the largest mosques in the world; its stunning architecture, with intricate mosaics and views over the Atlantic Ocean, will leave you in awe. Take a stroll along the Corniche, Casablanca’s beachfront promenade, lined with cafes and restaurants, then explore the Royal Palace and wander through the Old Medina, soaking in the local atmosphere and markets. Stay overnight in a modern hotel in Casablanca, preparing for the next day’s adventure.

OvernightModern hotel in CasablancaMeals—
Day 2Casablanca to Rabat – The Capital City

Travel from Casablanca to Rabat, Morocco’s capital. Known for its historical landmarks and beautiful gardens, Rabat offers a blend of history and tranquility. Visit Hassan Tower, a minaret that dates back to the 12th century, and the Mausoleum of Mohammed V, where Morocco’s royal family is buried. Explore the Kasbah of the Udayas, a peaceful fortress with narrow streets and gardens overlooking the Atlantic Ocean, and take a walk through the beautifully landscaped gardens near the Royal Palace. Enjoy an overnight stay in Rabat, with a chance to relax after a day of exploration.

OvernightRabatMealsBreakfast
Day 3Rabat to Chefchaouen – The Blue Pearl

Head towards Chefchaouen, the stunning “Blue City” located in the Rif Mountains. This picturesque town is famous for its blue-painted buildings and relaxed atmosphere. Spend time wandering its winding streets, where every building and alley is painted in shades of blue, visit the Ras El Ma Waterfall on the outskirts of town, offering a serene atmosphere surrounded by nature, and explore the Kasbah Museum, which features local artifacts and art. Overnight in Chefchaouen in a charming riad, enjoying the peaceful ambiance of the blue city.

OvernightCharming riad in ChefchaouenMealsBreakfast
Day 4Chefchaouen to Fes – The Cultural Heart of Morocco

Continue your journey south to Fes, one of Morocco’s oldest and most culturally significant cities. The medina of Fes is a UNESCO World Heritage site and a hub of Moroccan history and architecture. Explore the labyrinthine streets of the Fes Medina, home to traditional artisan workshops, local markets, and centuries-old buildings, visit the Al-Qarawiyyin University, one of the oldest continuously operating universities in the world, and marvel at the architecture of the Bou Inania Madrasa, a religious school with stunning mosaics and intricate woodwork. Stay overnight in a traditional riad within the medina, experiencing Moroccan hospitality at its best.

OvernightTraditional riad in the Fes medinaMealsBreakfast
Day 5Fes to Merzouga – Into the Sahara Desert

Depart Fes and head toward Merzouga, the gateway to the Sahara Desert. This leg of the journey takes you through the Middle Atlas Mountains and along scenic valleys, before arriving at the mesmerizing desert dunes of Erg Chebbi. Stop in Ifrane, often called “Little Switzerland,” before continuing through the Middle Atlas Mountains and the Ziz Valley. Arrive in Merzouga in the evening, where you will begin your Sahara adventure with a camel trek into the desert. Experience an unforgettable sunset over the sand dunes followed by a traditional Berber dinner at a desert camp, accompanied by local music and stargazing. Spend the night in a luxury desert camp, where you can experience the Sahara under the stars.

OvernightLuxury desert campMealsBreakfast · Dinner
Day 6Merzouga to Ouarzazate – Discovering Kasbahs and Valleys

After a night in the desert, head to Ouarzazate, a city renowned for its kasbahs and cinematic history. Visit the stunning Todra Gorge, a dramatic canyon with towering cliffs perfect for a short walk or photo opportunities. Continue through the Dades Valley and Roses Valley, famous for their beautiful landscapes and kasbahs, before reaching Ouarzazate, and visit Ait Ben Haddou, a UNESCO World Heritage site known for its iconic kasbahs that have appeared in numerous Hollywood films. Stay in a charming guesthouse or hotel in Ouarzazate, where you can relax after a full day of sightseeing.

OvernightGuesthouse or hotel in OuarzazateMealsBreakfast
Day 7Ouarzazate to Marrakech – The Red City

On your final day, travel from Ouarzazate to Marrakech, crossing the majestic Atlas Mountains along the way. Marrakech is a city of vibrant markets, historic palaces, and tranquil gardens. Drive through the Tizi n’Tichka Pass, a high mountain pass offering panoramic views of the Atlas Mountains. Upon arriving in Marrakech, visit the lively Jemaa el-Fna Square, where you can see street performers, food stalls, and artisans, and admire the beauty of the Koutoubia Mosque, a landmark of Marrakech. In the evening, your tour concludes with a transfer to the airport for your departure.
