Marrakech desert tours

Marrakech Desert Tours – From the Red City to the Sahara

Marrakech is the natural starting point for a journey into the Moroccan Sahara. In a few hours you leave the souks behind, cross the High Atlas over the Tizi n’Tichka pass and enter a world of earthen kasbahs, palm oases, deep gorges and golden dunes. With Morocco Adventures Tour, every desert trip from Marrakech is private, planned around your dates and led by a local team that knows every road of the south.

Why travel to the desert with us?

We are a Moroccan team, and the south is our home. We plan each route so you see more and rush less, with honest advice on how many days you really need.

  • Private tours only — your own vehicle and driver-guide
  • Departures any day, from your riad or hotel in Marrakech
  • Hand-picked riads, kasbah hotels and desert camps
  • Tours from 2 to 12 days, fully adaptable
  • A clear quote, with no hidden costs

What our desert tours include

Each tour page lists exactly what is included. In general, our desert tours from Marrakech come with:

  • Private air-conditioned vehicle with fuel
  • Experienced English or Spanish-speaking driver-guide
  • Nights in riads, hotels and a desert camp
  • Breakfasts and dinners, as shown on each tour
  • Camel ride into the dunes at sunset
  • Berber music around the fire at the camp

What you will live on the way

Camel rides at sunset and sunrise

Ride into the dunes as the sand turns from gold to deep red, then wake early to watch the sun rise over the Sahara.

A night under the stars

Dinner in the camp, mint tea, drums by the fire and a sky full of stars far from any city light.

Kasbahs and Berber villages

Walk through Ait Ben Haddou, the Skoura palm grove and earthen villages where daily life still follows old traditions.

Gorges and valleys

The Dades Valley and the Todra Gorge offer red cliffs, green fields and some of the most striking roads in Morocco.
