Tangier to Marrakech

8 Days Tour from Tangier to Marrakech

  • Duration8 days
  • StartTangier
  • EndMarrakech
  • PriceOn request
Overview Itinerary Included Map Book

Overview

Embark on an 8-day adventure from Tangier to Marrakech, discovering the beauty, culture, and history of Morocco. This tour takes you through the blue city of Chefchaouen, the historic medina of Fes, the magical Sahara Desert, and the majestic Atlas Mountains before arriving at the vibrant streets of Marrakech.

Highlights

  • 01Wander through the stunning blue streets of Chefchaouen
  • 02Explore the ancient medina of Fes, a UNESCO World Heritage Site
  • 03Experience a magical night in the Sahara Desert, complete with a camel trek
  • 04Visit the iconic Ait Ben Haddou, a famous ksar and filming location
  • 05Discover the lively souks, landmarks, and gardens of Marrakech

Itinerary

Tangier · Chefchaouen · Fes · Merzouga · Dades · Ouarzazate · Marrakech

Day 1Tangier to Chefchaouen – The Blue Pearl

Your tour begins in Tangier, where you’ll travel to the serene and picturesque town of Chefchaouen, nestled in the Rif Mountains. Known as the “Blue Pearl,” Chefchaouen enchants visitors with its unique charm and stunning views. Stroll through its blue-washed streets, relax at Plaza Uta el-Hammam, and enjoy panoramic views from Ras El Ma spring.

OvernightChefchaouenMeals—
Day 2Chefchaouen to Fes – Historical Wonders

Depart Chefchaouen and head to the imperial city of Fes, stopping en route to visit some historical sites. Explore the ancient Roman ruins of Volubilis, discover Meknes, another imperial city, and see landmarks like Bab Mansour, then arrive in Fes and enjoy your evening in this cultural capital.

OvernightFesMealsBreakfast
Day 3Fes – Dive into Morocco’s Cultural Heart

Spend the day uncovering the wonders of Fes, Morocco’s oldest imperial city and a treasure trove of history and culture. Visit the Al-Qarawiyyin University, the world’s oldest university, see the traditional Chouara Tanneries, and wander through the medina’s vibrant souks to admire the Bou Inania Madrasa.

OvernightFesMealsBreakfast
Day 4Fes to Merzouga – The Gateway to the Sahara

Leave Fes and travel to Merzouga, passing through picturesque landscapes of the Middle Atlas Mountains and lush valleys. Stop in Ifrane, a charming town known as “Little Switzerland,” visit the Azrou cedar forest and spot Barbary macaques, and drive through the stunning Ziz Valley and its palm groves. Arrive in Merzouga, where you’ll embark on a camel trek to a luxury desert camp and enjoy a night under the starry desert sky.

OvernightLuxury desert camp in MerzougaMealsBreakfast
Day 5Merzouga to Dades Valley – Desert Landscapes

Wake up early to watch the sunrise over the dunes before continuing your journey to the Dades Valley. Visit the breathtaking Todra Gorge, with its towering cliffs, and explore the scenic Dades Valley, known for its unique rock formations and lush surroundings.

OvernightDades ValleyMealsBreakfast
Day 6Dades Valley to Ouarzazate – The Road of a Thousand Kasbahs

Travel along the Road of a Thousand Kasbahs, stopping at fascinating sites along the way, including Morocco’s Hollywood. Visit the spectacular Ait Ben Haddou, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, and stop in Ouarzazate, famous for its film studios and the impressive Kasbah Taourirt.

OvernightOuarzazateMealsBreakfast
Day 7Ouarzazate to Marrakech – Over the High Atlas Mountains

Drive to Marrakech via the scenic Tizi n’Tichka Pass, one of Morocco’s highest mountain roads. Along the way, admire the stunning landscapes of the High Atlas Mountains, visit local Berber villages and marvel at the dramatic mountain scenery, then arrive in Marrakech, where the city’s vibrant energy awaits.

OvernightMarrakechMealsBreakfast
Day 8Explore Marrakech – The Red City

Spend your final day exploring Marrakech, a city known for its rich history, vibrant souks, and exquisite architecture. Stroll through the bustling Jemaa el-Fnaa Square, admire the stunning Koutoubia Mosque, visit the tranquil Majorelle Garden and learn about its connection to Yves Saint Laurent, and explore the opulent Bahia Palace and the ancient Saadian Tombs. Your 8-day adventure concludes in Marrakech, leaving you with unforgettable memories of Morocco.

Overnight—MealsBreakfast
