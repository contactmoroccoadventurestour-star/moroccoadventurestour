12 Days from Marrakech Imperial Cities & Sahara

  • Duration12 days · 11 nights
  • StartMarrakech

Overview Itinerary Included Map Book

Overview

Twelve days to see Morocco properly: the red city of Marrakech, the kasbahs of the south, the great dunes of the Sahara, the imperial cities of the north and the Atlantic capitals — at a relaxed pace, with time to stop and look.

You start with two days in Marrakech, including a guided walk through the medina and a visit to the Majorelle Garden. Then the road crosses the High Atlas to Ait Ben Haddou and Ouarzazate, and continues through the Rose Valley and the Todra Gorges to Merzouga.

In the Sahara you take your time: Gnawa music in Khamlia, a visit to a nomad family, and a camel trek into the Erg Chebbi dunes for a night in a desert camp, with sunset and sunrise over the sand.

The second half takes you north through the Ziz Valley, Midelt and the cedar forests of Azrou to Fes, then on to Volubilis, Meknes, Rabat and Casablanca, before a final free day back in Marrakech.

Itinerary

Marrakech · Ouarzazate · Merzouga · Midelt · Fes · Rabat · Casablanca · Marrakech

Day 1Welcome to Marrakech

We meet you at the airport and drive you to your hotel or riad. The rest of the day is yours to settle in and enjoy your first evening in the Red City.

OvernightHotel or riad in MarrakechMeals—
Day 2Marrakech with a local guide

A local guide meets you in the medina for a walking tour of the old town. After lunch in the heart of the medina, visit the Majorelle Garden before a free evening in Marrakech.

OvernightHotel or riad in MarrakechMealsBreakfast
Day 3Marrakech – Ait Ben Haddou – Ouarzazate

We cross the High Atlas over the Tizi n’Tichka pass to the kasbah of Ait Ben Haddou, then continue to Ouarzazate and its film studios. Night in Ouarzazate.

OvernightHotel in OuarzazateMealsBreakfast
Day 4Ouarzazate – Todra Gorges – Merzouga

Drive through the Rose Valley and stop at the Todra Gorges to walk between the high rock walls. In the evening you reach Merzouga, at the foot of the Erg Chebbi dunes.

OvernightHotel in MerzougaMealsBreakfast
Day 5Exploring the Merzouga area

A day around the desert: visit the village of Khamlia to listen to Gnawa music, meet a nomad family, discover palm-fringed oases and the landscapes around Erg Chebbi.

OvernightHotel in MerzougaMealsBreakfast
Day 6Camel trek & night in the desert

In the afternoon you ride camels into the highest dunes of Morocco to reach the camp for sunset. Dinner is a traditional Moroccan meal, followed by Berber drums around the fire and a night under a sky full of stars.

OvernightDesert camp in Erg ChebbiMealsBreakfast · Dinner
Day 7Merzouga – Rissani – Ziz Valley – Midelt

Sunrise over the dunes and breakfast at the camp. We head north through the market town of Rissani, Erfoud, the palm groves of the Ziz Valley and Errachidia to Midelt.

OvernightHotel in MideltMealsBreakfast
Day 8Midelt – Azrou cedar forest – Fes

Cross the Middle Atlas, stop in the cedar forest of Azrou to see the Barbary macaques, pass through Ifrane and arrive in Fes.

OvernightHotel or riad in FesMealsBreakfast
Day 9Fes with a local guide

Explore the medieval medina of Fes with a local guide, including the tanneries and the ceramics workshops. Night in a traditional riad.

OvernightRiad in FesMealsBreakfast
Day 10Fes – Volubilis – Meknes – Rabat

Visit the Roman ruins of Volubilis, have lunch in the imperial city of Meknes, then continue to Rabat, the capital, for the night.

OvernightHotel in RabatMealsBreakfast
Day 11Rabat – Casablanca – Marrakech

Discover the historic sites of Rabat, then drive along the Atlantic to Casablanca for lunch or a drink by the ocean, before the road back to Marrakech.

OvernightHotel or riad in MarrakechMealsBreakfast
Day 12Free day & departure

Time for last-minute shopping in the souks and packing, then we drive you to the airport for your flight home.

Overnight—MealsBreakfast
