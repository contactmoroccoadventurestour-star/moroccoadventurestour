Overview

Embark on a 12-day Moroccan journey that captures the essence of this vibrant and diverse country. From Casablanca’s iconic mosque to the bustling medinas of Marrakech, this adventure takes you through Morocco’s most celebrated landscapes, cities, and cultural highlights. Discover blue-painted Chefchaouen, immerse yourself in Fes’s ancient history, trek through the Sahara Desert, and experience the charm of Marrakech.

A 12-day trip provides a perfect balance of exploration and relaxation. You’ll have time to delve into Morocco’s rich culture, enjoy its breathtaking natural beauty, and explore at a comfortable pace without feeling rushed.

Itinerary

Casablanca · Rabat · Chefchaouen · Fes · Merzouga · Ouarzazate · Marrakech · Essaouira

Day 1Arrival in Casablanca – The Starting Point

Welcome to Morocco! Upon arrival at Casablanca Mohammed V International Airport, you’ll meet your guide and driver who will introduce you to Morocco’s largest city. Admire the Hassan II Mosque, an architectural masterpiece and one of the largest mosques in the world, located right by the Atlantic Ocean. Stroll along the Corniche Ain Diab, the waterfront promenade, with restaurants and views of the ocean, and enjoy a meal at Rick’s Café, the iconic restaurant inspired by the movie Casablanca. Spend the night in Casablanca, resting for the adventure ahead.

OvernightCasablancaMeals—
Day 2Casablanca to Rabat – Morocco’s Capital

After breakfast, travel to Rabat, Morocco’s capital and a UNESCO World Heritage city known for its history and coastal charm. Admire the grandeur of the Royal Palace, home to the King of Morocco, wander through the tranquil streets of the Kasbah of the Udayas, a fortress overlooking the Bou Regreg River, visit the Mausoleum of Mohammed V, the resting place of King Mohammed V and his sons, and discover the Hassan Tower, the iconic minaret of an incomplete 12th-century mosque. Stay overnight in Rabat.

OvernightRabatMealsBreakfast
Day 3Rabat to Chefchaouen – The Blue Pearl

Journey north to the stunning Chefchaouen, a town famous for its blue-painted streets and mountain views. Stroll through the picturesque alleys of the blue medina, capturing breathtaking photographs of the vibrant blue architecture, relax at the peaceful Ras El Ma spring with views of the Rif Mountains, and explore small artisan shops selling handmade textiles, leather goods, and pottery. Enjoy a serene evening in Chefchaouen.

OvernightChefchaouenMealsBreakfast
Day 4Chefchaouen to Fes – The Cultural Capital

After breakfast, travel to Fes, Morocco’s oldest imperial city and spiritual heart, with a scenic drive through the Rif Mountains and rural towns. Stop in Volubilis, the ancient Roman ruins, to explore well-preserved mosaics and arches, and visit Meknes, another imperial city, to see landmarks like the Bab Mansour Gate and the Royal Stables. Arrive in Fes for an overnight stay in a traditional riad.

OvernightTraditional riad in FesMealsBreakfast
Day 5Full-Day Exploration of Fes

Spend a full day exploring the wonders of Fes, known for its medieval medina and cultural heritage. Wander through Fes el-Bali, a UNESCO-listed old town with narrow alleys, vibrant souks, and historic landmarks, visit the Al-Qarawiyyin University, the world’s oldest university, marvel at the intricate architecture of the Bou Inania Madrasa, and watch traditional leather-dyeing methods at the Chouara Tanneries. Spend another night in Fes.

OvernightFesMealsBreakfast
Day 6Fes to Merzouga – Gateway to the Sahara

Depart early for Merzouga, the gateway to the Sahara Desert, with scenic stops and cultural landmarks along the way. Stop in Ifrane, Morocco’s “Little Switzerland,” known for its Alpine-style architecture, spot Barbary macaque monkeys in the tranquil Azrou cedar forest, and admire the palm groves and lush landscapes of the Ziz Valley. Ride camels into the Erg Chebbi dunes and watch a magical sunset. Spend the night in a luxury desert camp, enjoying Berber music under the starry sky.

OvernightLuxury desert campMealsBreakfast
Day 7Merzouga to Todra Gorge

Wake up early to watch the sunrise over the dunes before continuing your journey to the dramatic Todra Gorge. Hike through its towering red cliffs and enjoy stunning views, and visit Berber villages along the way. Stay overnight in a charming hotel near the gorge.

OvernightHotel near the Todra GorgeMealsBreakfast
Day 8Todra Gorge to Ouarzazate

Travel through the Dades Valley, also known as the “Valley of a Thousand Kasbahs,” to reach Ouarzazate, Morocco’s filmmaking hub. Admire the dramatic rock formations of the Dades Gorges, stop in the Rose Valley, known for its fragrant roses and rosewater production, and explore the palm groves and historic kasbahs of the Skoura Oasis. Stay in Ouarzazate.

OvernightOuarzazateMealsBreakfast
Day 9Ouarzazate to Marrakech

On the way to Marrakech, visit iconic landmarks and enjoy scenic mountain views. Explore Ait Ben Haddou, a UNESCO-listed ksar and backdrop for many famous movies, and enjoy panoramic views of the Atlas Mountains from the Tizi n’Tichka Pass. Arrive in Marrakech for an overnight stay.

OvernightMarrakechMealsBreakfast
Day 10Full-Day Exploration of Marrakech

Dive into the vibrant atmosphere of Marrakech, known as the “Red City.” Experience the lively Jemaa el-Fna Square, filled with food stalls, performers, and vendors, explore the lush Majorelle Gardens created by Jacques Majorelle, admire the intricate design and beautiful courtyards of the historic Bahia Palace, and visit the Koutoubia, the city’s largest mosque. Stay another night in Marrakech.

OvernightMarrakechMealsBreakfast
Day 11Day Trip to Essaouira

Take a day trip to the coastal city of Essaouira, known for its laid-back charm and Atlantic views. Wander through the charming medina with its white-and-blue buildings, walk along the historic ramparts of the Skala de la Kasbah overlooking the ocean, and savor fresh catches at the harbor. Return to Marrakech for an overnight stay.

OvernightMarrakechMealsBreakfast
Day 12Departure from Casablanca

Transfer back to Casablanca for your departure, taking with you unforgettable memories of Morocco.

Overnight—MealsBreakfast
