Overview

Embark on a 9-day Morocco adventure starting from the vibrant city of Casablanca. This journey will immerse you in Morocco’s rich culture, stunning landscapes, and unforgettable experiences. From the bustling souks to the serene desert dunes, this itinerary takes you through the country’s most iconic destinations, including Fes, Chefchaouen, Marrakech, and the Merzouga Desert, offering a perfect balance of history, adventure, and relaxation.

A 9-day Morocco itinerary allows you to see a wide range of destinations, from modern cities and historic landmarks to the natural beauty of the desert. With ample time to explore, you’ll have the opportunity to experience Morocco’s rich culture and traditions. Whether you’re fascinated by ancient medinas, eager to ride a camel through the Sahara Desert, or simply want to immerse yourself in Moroccan architecture, this itinerary has it all.

Itinerary

Casablanca · Rabat · Chefchaouen · Fes · Merzouga · Dades · Ouarzazate · Marrakech

Day 1Arrival in Casablanca – Morocco’s Modern Gateway

Start your adventure in Casablanca, the bustling economic heart of Morocco. This cosmopolitan city blends modernity with traditional charm. Visit the grand Hassan II Mosque, one of the largest mosques in the world, explore the Royal Palace and take a relaxing walk along the Corniche, with views of the Atlantic Ocean, and discover the Mahkama du Pacha, a beautifully decorated government building. Stay in Casablanca overnight, getting ready for your next destination.

OvernightCasablancaMeals—
Day 2Casablanca to Rabat – The Capital City

Head to Rabat, the capital of Morocco, known for its historical sites and lush gardens. Visit the iconic Hassan Tower, an incomplete minaret dating back to the 12th century, explore the Mausoleum of Mohammed V, the resting place of Morocco’s royal family, and wander through the Kasbah of the Udayas, a fortress with panoramic views of the Atlantic Ocean. Stay overnight in Rabat and enjoy its peaceful, less touristy atmosphere.

OvernightRabatMealsBreakfast
Day 3Rabat to Chefchaouen – The Blue Pearl

Journey to Chefchaouen, the stunning “Blue City” nestled in the Rif Mountains. Stroll through its iconic blue-painted streets, offering perfect photo opportunities, enjoy a visit to the Ras El Ma waterfall, a scenic spot where the water flows through the city, and explore the Kasbah, a fortified building with a museum showcasing local culture. Overnight in Chefchaouen, surrounded by the calm beauty of the blue city.

OvernightChefchaouenMealsBreakfast
Day 4Chefchaouen to Fes – The Cultural Heart of Morocco

Travel to Fes, one of Morocco’s oldest cities and a center of Islamic culture and learning. Discover the winding alleys and historic sites of the Fes Medina, a UNESCO World Heritage site, visit the Al-Qarawiyyin University, one of the oldest universities in the world, and admire the architecture of the Bou Inania Madrasa, a stunning example of Islamic craftsmanship. Stay in a traditional riad in Fes for an authentic Moroccan experience.

OvernightTraditional riad in FesMealsBreakfast
Day 5Fes to Merzouga – The Sahara Desert

Head towards Merzouga, your gateway to the Sahara Desert, through scenic landscapes and picturesque towns. Stop in Ifrane, known for its Swiss-like architecture, and continue through the Middle Atlas Mountains. Marvel at the Ziz Valley, home to beautiful palm groves and Berber villages. Arrive in Merzouga and embark on a camel trek through the golden dunes of Erg Chebbi. Watch the desert sunset and enjoy a traditional dinner under the stars. Spend the night in a luxury desert camp, experiencing the serenity of the Sahara Desert.

OvernightLuxury desert campMealsBreakfast · Dinner
Day 6Merzouga to Todra Gorge – Natural Wonders

Leave the desert behind and head towards the impressive Todra Gorge and the Dades Valley, known for their stunning landscapes. Hike through the Todra Gorge, a dramatic canyon with towering cliffs perfect for adventure seekers, continue to the Dades Valley, with its captivating views and traditional kasbahs, and stop to admire the unique Kasbahs of the Roses, which offer a glimpse into Morocco’s ancient architecture. Stay overnight in the Dades Valley or a nearby town, soaking in the tranquility of the area.

OvernightDades ValleyMealsBreakfast
Day 7Todra Gorge to Ouarzazate – The Hollywood of Morocco

Make your way to Ouarzazate, known as the “Hollywood of Morocco” due to its role in film productions. Visit Ait Ben Haddou, a UNESCO World Heritage site and famous kasbah featured in many films like “Gladiator,” and explore the Ouarzazate Film Studios, where iconic movies like “Lawrence of Arabia” were filmed. Stay in Ouarzazate, where you can enjoy the desert-like atmosphere of the region.

OvernightOuarzazateMealsBreakfast
Day 8Ouarzazate to Marrakech – The Red City

Head towards Marrakech, the vibrant, bustling city that’s famous for its souks, palaces, and gardens. Travel through the Tizi n’Tichka Pass in the Atlas Mountains, offering breathtaking views. Upon arrival in Marrakech, explore the vibrant Jemaa el-Fna Square, known for its street performers, food stalls, and local crafts, and visit the Koutoubia Mosque, the largest mosque in Marrakech, with its towering minaret. Overnight in Marrakech, ready to explore more the following day.

OvernightMarrakechMealsBreakfast
Day 9Marrakech – Departure

On your final day, you’ll have some free time to enjoy Marrakech before departing. Visit the Majorelle Gardens, a botanical oasis with vibrant colors and tranquility, explore the beautiful Bahia Palace, showcasing Moroccan architecture and lush gardens, and shop for souvenirs and Moroccan handicrafts in the lively souks. After an enriching 9-day journey, you’ll be transferred to the airport for your departure.

Overnight—MealsBreakfast
